// 001 — Mission

Built by students.
Driven by data.

The IUB Formula Team is a multidisciplinary senior project group from Independent University, Bangladesh. Students from Mechanical, Electrical, and Computer Engineering collaborate end-to-end — from CAD models to real-world track testing.

Every component is designed, analysed, fabricated and validated by the team. No outsourcing, no shortcuts.
